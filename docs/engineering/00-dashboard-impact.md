## 4. Decisions (answered 14 Nov 2026)

- **D1 — Condition:** keep it as an optional dropdown (New / Refurbished / Graded / Ex-display), defaulting to **New**.
- **D2 — Product actions:** two clear buttons on every product, "Ask on WhatsApp" and "Email us", prefilled with the product name. **No enquiry form.**
- **D3 — Deleting a category with products:** blocked, with a plain message telling the owner what to do.
- **D4 — Web address:** a product's slug never changes after its first publish.
- **D5 — 3D bed story:** stays as its own page and is **not** in the shopping path. Clicking a menu category goes straight to its products. How visitors reach the story is proposed in Phase 4(d).
- **D6 — Who sells:** suppliers sell and dispatch, and the owner showcases. "Supplier" and "referral" must not dominate the site. The disclosure is honest, minimal and positive: one line on How it works, on Terms, and next to the contact buttons, e.g. *"We work with trusted UK suppliers who dispatch your order directly."* No supplier names per product.

## 4b. Main UX goal (owner's answer to "what feels confusing", 14 Nov 2026)

The whole site must be easy for a non-technical person, both the visitor and the owner.
- A first-time visitor understands what the business is within **5 seconds** of opening the homepage.
- Any product is reachable in **at most 2 clicks**.
- **One** simple header row, plain words instead of jargon, big clear buttons, and far fewer homepage sections than today's 9.
- The owner can add a product **without help or instructions**.

## 4c. What a first-time visitor does first (owner's answer, 14 Nov 2026)

- **Browse visually first**: pick a category from **large picture tiles** (using the Category Image), or look at a **few featured products** chosen by the owner (the Featured on homepage flag).
- **End goal**: the visitor finds a product they like and presses **"Ask on WhatsApp"**.
- **Search** stays available but **small, in the header**, for people who already know what they want. It is not a big homepage search block.
- **WhatsApp** is easy to reach on **every product**, but it is **not the first thing** on the homepage.

## 4d. Success a few weeks after launch (owner's answer, 14 Nov 2026)

1. The owner adds **at least 10 products in the first week**, without help, **each in under 5 minutes**.
2. WhatsApp enquiries from the site are counted by the owner from the prefilled message text. No analytics or tracking. **No target yet**: set one after the first month of real data.
3. Homepage and product pages load in **about 3 s on a phone over 4G**. The 3D bed page is excluded unless `bed.glb` is compressed first.
4. **No page ever goes blank**, even when a CDN or 3D asset fails.
5. Launch: **no fixed date**. Launch when the success criteria and the pre-launch checklist are met.

## 5. Proposed CLAUDE.md change (applied only after your approval)

**§1 Business model**, replacing the "No backend / no admin dashboard" and "Deal actions" bullets:
> - **Owner dashboard + API**: A small Express API (`/server`) with one owner login stores categories and products. The owner manages them in a mobile-friendly dashboard. The public site reads only **Published** products and **Show in menu** categories from the API.
> - **Product actions**: Each product offers "Ask on WhatsApp" (`+44 7344 540043`) and "Email us", prefilled with the product name. No cart, no checkout, no on-site payments, ever.
> - **Categories are data**, not code: the menu is built from published categories in the owner's menu order.

**§2 rule 1**, replacing "No extra packages":
> The exact dependency lists above are the approved set. Adding a package requires an ADR in `docs/engineering/04-architecture.md` and the owner's approval, and both lists here must be updated in the same commit. Prefer Node built-ins (`node:crypto` scrypt, `node:test`) over packages.

**§6:** add `/server`, `/shared` and the dashboard folder, and remove `suppliers.ts`, `leadFlow.ts` and `SharedProductPreviewCanvas.tsx`.

**§7**, replaced with "Product & category data model": the fields in §3 above, plus these rules:
- Only Published products are public.
- Price is hidden when empty.
- `Product` JSON-LD `Offer` only when Published and a price is set.
- The sitemap is generated from Published data.
- IDs, slugs and dates are never shown in any UI.

**§9**, replacing the "Respect the referral business model" bullet:
> Never add a cart, checkout, payment gateway or customer accounts. The only login is the single owner account. Dashboard labels use plain UK English with no technical terms. Every field is validated on the server, whatever the dashboard checked.

**§10 item 1:** "Owner adds real products and photos in the dashboard. Delete all sample data before launch."

**§11:** add a change-log entry when this lands.

**Added to the dependency lists, pending Phase 4:** to be filled in once the hosting and database choice is approved, with the smallest set possible.
